# Privacy Policy for Bubble Level (com.gowmaz.blevel)

Last updated: May 08, 2026

This privacy policy describes how **Gowmaz** ("we", "us", or "our") handles information through the **Bubble Level** mobile application (the "App").

## 1. No Data Collection
The App is designed with a privacy-first architecture. **We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or telemetry.**

*   **No Personal Information**: We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, location, or any other personal identifiers.
*   **No Analytics**: The App does not contain any third-party tracking or analytics SDKs (such as Firebase or Google Analytics).
*   **No Advertising**: The App does not use any advertising IDs or track your behavior for marketing purposes.

## 2. Permissions and Device Sensors
The App requires specific permissions and access to device sensors to function as a measurement tool:

*   **Accelerometer**: The App accesses your device's accelerometer sensor to calculate orientation, pitch, and roll. This data is processed locally in real-time on your device and is never stored or transmitted.
*   **Wake Lock**: The App uses the `WAKE_LOCK` permission to prevent the screen from dimming while you are performing active measurements.
*   **Local Storage**: The App saves your calibration settings (offsets) and UI preferences locally on your device using `SharedPreferences`. This data remains on your device at all times and is not accessible by us.

## 3. Offline Operation
The App does **not** require or request the `INTERNET` permission. All processing happens entirely offline on your physical device. No data ever leaves your device.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Because the App operates entirely offline and does not collect data, no information is shared with third-party services.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us via our GitHub repository: [https://github.com/wazhanudin/Level](https://github.com/wazhanudin/Level)
